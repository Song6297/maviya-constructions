About Bricks & Bits

Empowering construction professionals with smart, simple tools to manage projects efficiently.

Our Story

B&B (Bricks & Bits) was born from a simple observation: construction professionals deserve better tools. While the construction industry builds the world around us, many contractors and builders still rely on paper notebooks, scattered spreadsheets, and memory to track their projects.


We set out to change that. B&B is a comprehensive construction management platform designed specifically for Indian contractors, builders, and construction companies. From tracking materials and labor costs to managing payments and generating reports, B&B brings everything together in one simple, powerful app.

What We Offer

Project Management
Track multiple construction projects with budgets, timelines, and progress all in one place.
Material Tracking
Monitor material purchases, usage, and costs. Never lose track of where your money goes.
Labor Management
Track worker attendance, payments, and assignments across all your projects.
Payment Tracking
Record all payments - to vendors, workers, and from clients. Know your cash flow instantly.
Reports & Analytics
Generate detailed reports and compare projects to make better business decisions.
Works Everywhere
Access your data from any device - phone, tablet, or computer. Your office is wherever you are.
