Frequently Asked Questions

What is B&B - Bricks & Bits?
B&B (Bricks & Bits) is a comprehensive construction management platform designed for Indian businesses. It helps you track projects, manage materials, handle payments, and monitor finances - all in one place.
What's new in version 2.1?
Version 2.1 introduces the Labour Calendar system for daily work tracking, company branding with logo upload, phase management, vendor management, and improved settings. Premium users also get Fund Management.
How does the Labour Calendar work?
The Labour Calendar lets you track daily work entries for each labourer. You can mark attendance (Present/Half-Day/Absent), record payments, and see which dates are paid vs unpaid. It uses a FIFO system to track payment status.
How much does B&B cost?
Free users pay ₹5 per project creation. Premium users pay ₹199/month for unlimited projects and access to all premium features including Fund Management, Floor Plans, and Documents.
Is my data secure?
Yes! B&B uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore for secure data storage. Your data is encrypted and stored in Google's secure cloud infrastructure.
Can I upload my company logo?
Yes! Go to Settings > Account and upload your company logo. It will appear on the Hub, Dashboard, and Labour Calendar banners. Images are automatically compressed to ensure fast loading.
Can I access B&B on mobile?
Yes! B&B is fully responsive and works on all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile. Access your projects from anywhere, anytime.
How do I upgrade to Premium?
Go to the Upgrade page from your dashboard, make the payment via UPI, and submit your payment details. Once verified by admin, your premium features will be activated.
What features are included in Premium?
Premium includes: Unlimited projects, Fund Management, Floor Plans viewer, Document storage, Priority support, and all future premium features.
